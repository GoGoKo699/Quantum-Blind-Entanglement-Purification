# Scientific reading route

The problem is to improve a noisy specimen of an unknown entangled target while preserving that target. Three local specimens provide a multiplicity degree of freedom that can reject noise while carrying the physical spin representation into the output.

| Step | Central question | Read |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is unknown, what is promised, and what does success mean? | [Model and exact claims](model.md) |
| 2 | Which physical local measurements implement the method? | [Protocol and covariance](protocol.md) |
| 3 | Why does the accepted state improve, including unequal qualities? | [Exact contraction and floor theorem](algebra.md) |
| 4 | Why does an imaginary relative phase succeed where real filters are neutral? | [Chiral multiplicity](chirality.md) |
| 5 | What is paid when successful outputs are recursively regrouped? | [Finite budgets, storage and time](resources.md) |
| 6 | How do the fixed local maps become elementary gates? | [Local circuit compiler](implementation.md) |
| 7 | What survives imperfect controls and finite precision? | [Complete-instrument error bounds](robustness.md) |
| 8 | What physical capability and limitations remain? | [Interpretation](interpretation.md) |

For attribution and model-level differences, read [related work](../references/RELATED_WORK.md). For reproduction, read [verification](../verification/README.md). The scientific account owns the exact assumptions and proofs; numerical residuals alone do not replace them.
